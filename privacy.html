The Mutyrõ app

  • No account, no server. You don't sign up and there is no Mutyrõ server that stores family data. We, the makers of Mutyrõ, receive no data from the app.
  • Family data stays on your devices. The family nickname, nicknames, roles, children's ages, languages, tasks, stars, rewards and treasure-chest toys are stored only on the devices of your family. The family code is kept in the device's Keychain.
  • Sync between your devices. Devices of the same family that are close together exchange their data directly with each other (Apple Multipeer Connectivity over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth), encrypted, and only after proving they know the family code. This is why iOS asks for "Local Network" access. Nothing goes over the internet.
  • Photo plan. A room photo is shrunk in memory and analysed by Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence). It never leaves your device, and Mutyrõ doesn't store it. A before/after photo is saved to your Photos app only when you tap "Save to Photos".
  • Camera. Used only to scan the family QR code and to photograph a room for the photo plan.
  • Face ID / Touch ID. Used only to let grown-ups into the parent area. The check happens in iOS; Mutyrõ never sees face or fingerprint data.
  • Notifications are created on the device itself. There is no push server.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking. The app contains no third-party code that collects data.
  • Purchases. Mutyrõ is free to use. The optional Mutyrõ Premium subscription is sold through Apple's App Store: Apple handles the payment and payment details, and we never see them. The app only checks with Apple, on the device, whether a subscription is active, and shares that status with your family's other devices when they sync. We receive no purchase data that identifies you; Apple sends us anonymous sales reports.

Deleting your data

In Family settings, "Disconnect this device" removes the family from that device, and "Delete our family on all devices" removes it from every family device in reach. Deleting the app also removes its data from that device. There is no copy with us.

Children

Parents create and manage children's profiles. Real names aren't needed; nicknames are enough. Children can't make purchases, open web links or contact anyone from the app; the parent area is protected by a grown-ups-only gate.

Emails to us

If you write to us, we use your email only to answer you, and delete it once the conversation is finished.

This website

The website sets no cookies, uses no tracking and loads no fonts or scripts from other servers. The language you pick is remembered only in your browser (localStorage).

Hosting: the website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open a page, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address in its server logs to deliver the page and keep it secure (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability and objection, and you can complain to a data-protection authority. Because we hold no family data, most requests are answered by the delete options in the app. For anything else, write to hi@mutyro.app.
