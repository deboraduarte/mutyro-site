Starting is the hardest part. So Mutyrõ makes the start tiny.

When a room feels too big to begin, “just tidy up” doesn’t help. Mutyrõ gives you one small thing to do, like “Put the blanket on the sofa”, and a 2-minute timer. Stopping after that counts. Usually you keep going.

For the whole family

Tidying, as a game you play together

Every person gets their own board, in their own language. Stars go to one shared team, so kids work together instead of arguing about who did more.

🧒

Kid boards

Up to 5 tasks with pictures and stars for big kids (6–10). Little ones (3–5) get 3 big picture tiles with no numbers. Every tile reads itself aloud.

👍

A parent’s OK

Kids tick a task and hear a happy sound. Stars count after a parent’s OK. “Not yet” puts it back on the list, and no star is ever taken away.

⏱️

Tidy-up races

2, 5 or 10 minutes, with music and the same countdown on every family phone. Nobody loses: the macaw always reaches its nest, and every racer gets stars.

🍕

A family reward

Choose a reward like Pizza night and a star goal. Everyone sees the bar fill up, and every phone celebrates when you get there.

🧸

Treasure chest

Toys go “on holiday” in the chest. Team stars earn swap tickets: one toy comes back, another goes on holiday. Fewer toys out, less mess.

🔐

A parent gate

Kids can play on a shared tablet. Settings and approvals open only with a parent’s Face ID, or by holding a button for 2 seconds.

Privacy

Your family stays in your home

Mutyrõ has no server that holds your family. Not your kids’ names, not your tasks, not your photos.

  • 👤
    No accountNo email, no password, no sign-up. Real names aren’t needed: nicknames are fine.
  • 📡
    Phones sync with each otherFamily phones and tablets that are close together sync directly over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, even without internet.
  • 🔑
    A family code, never sentA new phone joins with your family’s 16-character code or its QR code. Devices prove they know it without ever sending it.
  • 🗑️
    Delete everything, everywhereOne button removes your family from every family device. There’s no copy left anywhere else.

Made for real family life

Especially helpful if you or your kids find it hard to get started, with or without an ADHD diagnosis. Kind words only: no streaks to lose, no guilt, at most 2 notifications a day.

Questions

Which devices does Mutyrõ run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. The photo plan needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on; without it you can split a room by hand.

What happens if we lose a phone?

Every family device has a full copy, so nothing is lost while one other device has the family. Add the new phone with the family code and it copies everything.

Do the phones need to be together to sync?

Yes, they sync when they are near each other with Mutyrõ open, for example at home in the evening. No internet is needed.

Is the photo sent anywhere?

No. Apple’s on-device model reads it on your iPhone. Mutyrõ doesn’t keep it. A before/after photo is saved to your Photos only if you tap Save.
